---
openapi: get /v1/listening/recent/albums
---

Spelling aliases for the same artist and album appear once, at the position of
their newest play. Existing artwork and catalog links from compatible aliases
are retained. Different release qualifiers and conflicting MusicBrainz or
catalog identities stay separate. This groups the response without merging or
deleting stored listening history. Separately, verified album-artist filing can
reassign a play's album to its catalog compilation or collaboration, with an
audit record; it preserves the recording artist and other album plays.
