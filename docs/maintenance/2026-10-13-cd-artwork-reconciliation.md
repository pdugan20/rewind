## Review corrections

Two fresh `gpt-6-luna` reviewers at medium effort covered state/auth/publication and rendering/recovery, with primary-agent judgment. They found two concrete defects:

- Generic fallback public-read indexes were written before the descriptor compare-and-swap. A losing candidate could become publicly retrievable. Authorization now follows a winning descriptor write; same-selected-hash retries repair interrupted authorization. Regressions cover a losing write and an interruption after publication, while existing tests preserve previously published hashes.
- Legacy manual registration replaced source alpha with its own silhouette. It now interpolates premultiplied color and retains source alpha under that silhouette. Geometry measurement composites onto white so hidden RGB cannot establish a disc. Regressions cover translucent material, fully transparent hidden pixels, and rejection of a huge PNG header before decoding.

The shared decoder now bounds encoded input at 12 MiB and raster size at 12 megapixels; JPEG uses its decoder's resolution bound. The offline evaluator checks file size before reading. Private manifest paths remain operator-selected; this utility does not fetch or publish them.

Both reviewers rechecked the fixes and found no residual material blocker. The native-render cache reproduces bytes from the bound source and checks strategy/source/raw/material hashes. An unproven possibility of historical already-normalized raw input was not treated as an established defect.
